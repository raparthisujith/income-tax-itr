Result

Tax payable, computed both ways

Basis: Finance Act 2025 rates for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) — new regime slabs of ₹0–4L nil, 5%/10%/15%/20%/25%/30% thereafter with a ₹75,000 standard deduction and Section 87A rebate up to ₹60,000 (nil tax to ₹12L); old regime unchanged with a ₹50,000 standard deduction and ₹12,500 rebate up to ₹5L. Health & education cess of 4% applies to both. Surcharge (10%/15%/25%, capped at 25% in the new regime) is applied where total income exceeds ₹50L, without marginal relief — near a threshold, actual tax may be a little lower than shown. Section 87A rebate is applied only against tax on normal-rate income, not against Sec 111A/112A capital gains tax. Under the new regime a house-property loss cannot be set off against other heads, and Chapter VI-A deductions (other than 80CCD(2)/80JJAA) aren't available at all. Section 80GG and 80D caps are simplified. This sheet is for planning only — it isn't tax advice, and doesn't replace a CA's sign-off on your actual return.
